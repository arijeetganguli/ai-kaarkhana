# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AI Kaarkhana is a place for engineers to learn from each other. We want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where the project is represented, to be treated with respect and to feel able to contribute, whatever their experience level, background, identity or the tools they prefer.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** People write from different contexts and first languages. Ask before assuming.
- **Criticise ideas and work, never people.** "This step skips the failing test" is feedback. "You clearly do not understand testing" is not.
- **Teach, do not gatekeep.** A beginner's question is a chance to improve the documentation.
- **Be honest about what you know.** Say when you are unsure, when something is untested, or when AI helped you write it.
- **Credit others.** Cite sources and acknowledge contributions.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not share other people's personal information, and never post secrets, credentials or confidential company material.
- **Disagree constructively.** Engineering decisions involve trade offs. Explain your reasoning, listen to the answer, and accept the maintainers' decision on scope.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation, in any form.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or demeaning language about a person's identity.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Submitting content you do not have the right to share, or that you know to be false or deliberately misleading.
- Using the project to promote products or services in a way unrelated to its purpose.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the project maintainers. Use the contact method shown on the repository's profile, or open a private report through GitHub's *Report a vulnerability* or moderation features if they are enabled. Please include what happened, where, and any links or screenshots that help. Do not post the report publicly.

Maintainers will treat reports confidentially, will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith, and will respond as promptly as volunteers can.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate, including:

1. A private or public reminder of the expectations.
2. Editing or removing comments, issues, pull requests or other content.
3. A temporary restriction on taking part.
4. A permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest in a report step aside from deciding on it.
